About Us

Founded with a deeply compassionate vision to support abandoned and lonely elders, Sai Senior Citizens Caring Foundation offers a peaceful, nurturing environment equipped with modern healthcare and heartfelt companionship.

Our Leadership

Managed by a dedicated team of empathetic medical professionals, social workers, and caring trustees who treat every resident like their own family.

Our Facilities

Sunlit clean rooms, round-the-clock medical assistance, wholesome dining spaces, and peaceful green meditation gardens.

Mission, Vision and Values

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, compassionate care and a secure, loving shelter for senior citizens who lack family support systems.

Our Vision

A caring society where every elder is profoundly valued, respected, and embraced with warmth and dignity.

Core Values

Compassion: Caring deeply with empathy. | Respect: Honoring a lifetime of senior experiences. | Transparency: Complete honesty and trust in all operations.

Programs and Services

We offer specialized care designed to keep our residents physically active, medically secure, and mentally joyful.

Medical Care: 24/7 attentive nursing care, routine health screenings, and professional emergency management.
Nutritious Meals: Dietitian-approved, wholesome balanced meals prepared fresh daily with love and hygiene.
Recreation: Gentle yoga sessions, indoor games, book reading circles, and lively social gatherings.
Counseling & Support: Empathetic emotional backing to keep residents mentally active, connected, and happy.
Physiotherapy: Tailored gentle physical movement routines for safe joint mobility and pain management.

Please review our core community guidelines prior to acceptance:
  • Respect & Dignity: Treat all senior residents with utmost patience, kindness, and emotional care.
  • Privacy & Photography: Taking photographs or recording videos without prior staff approval is strictly prohibited.
  • Security & Identity Verification: All visitors/volunteers must present a valid Government photo ID upon entry.
  • Health & Food Safety: Outside food, drinks, or medications must NOT be handed directly to residents without permission.
  • Property & Safety Guidelines: Volunteers must stay within designated public/common areas and adhere to staff instructions.
